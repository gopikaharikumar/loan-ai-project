# Personal Loan Lending Policy

Sahyadri Retail Bank (fictional) | Version 1.0 | Educational prototype only. Not financial, medical or legal advice.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

**1.1** This policy governs decisions on unsecured personal loans made by Sahyadri Retail Bank.

**1.2** The loan amount must be between ₹50,000 and ₹25,00,000.

**1.3** The loan tenure must be between 12 and 60 months.

**1.4** The bank uses a statistical risk model that estimates the probability of default (PD) for each application. The model supports the decision but does not replace this policy.

**1.5** Where this policy and the risk model point in different directions, Section 6 applies.

## 2. Eligibility

**2.1** The applicant must be between 21 and 65 years old at the time of application.

**2.2** The applicant's reported monthly income must be at least ₹15,000. An application below this level is not eligible.

**2.3** A salaried applicant with less than 1 year in the current job must be sent for manual review.

**2.4** A self-employed applicant or business owner with less than 2 years in the current business must be sent for manual review.

**2.5** The accepted employment types are salaried, self-employed and business owner.

## 3. Credit History Rules

**3.1** A credit score of 750 or above is classed as strong.

**3.2** A credit score from 700 to 749 is classed as good.

**3.3** A credit score from 650 to 699 is classed as fair and is eligible for approval only when the debt-to-income ratio is 0.40 or below.

**3.4** A credit score from 600 to 649 is classed as weak. It must be sent for manual review and is eligible only for a loan of up to ₹5,00,000 with a debt-to-income ratio of 0.40 or below.

**3.5** A credit score below 600 is not eligible for an unsecured personal loan.

**3.6** An applicant with 3 or more past delinquencies is not eligible.

**3.7** An applicant with exactly 2 past delinquencies must be sent for manual review, whatever the credit score.

**3.8** An applicant with exactly 1 past delinquency and a credit score below 700 must be sent for manual review.

**3.9** An applicant with a credit history shorter than 1 year, or with no credit score, is a thin-file applicant. A thin-file applicant cannot be approved automatically.

**3.10** A thin-file applicant may be approved after manual review only for a loan of up to ₹2,00,000 with a debt-to-income ratio of 0.35 or below.

**3.11** An applicant with a credit history of 1 year or longer but no credit score on file must be sent for manual review to check for a data error.

## 4. Affordability Rules

**4.1** The debt-to-income ratio (DTI) is the sum of existing monthly EMI and the proposed monthly EMI, divided by monthly income.

**4.2** A DTI of 0.40 or below is within the standard limit.

**4.3** A DTI above 0.40 and up to 0.50 is allowed only when the credit score is 700 or above, and it must be sent for manual review.

**4.4** A DTI above 0.50 is not eligible, whatever the credit score or income.

**4.5** The loan-to-income ratio is the loan amount divided by annual income (monthly income multiplied by 12).

**4.6** A loan-to-income ratio above 2.0 is not eligible.

**4.7** A loan-to-income ratio above 1.5 and up to 2.0 must be sent for manual review.

**4.8** A reported monthly income above ₹5,00,000 must be verified against documents by a loan officer before it is used in any calculation.

## 5. Risk Tiers from the Risk Model

**5.1** The risk model outputs a probability of default (PD) between 0 and 1 for each application.

**5.2** An application with a PD below the approval cut-off of 0.10 is low risk.

**5.3** An application with a PD from 0.10 up to the rejection cut-off of 0.25 is borderline and must be sent for manual review.

**5.4** An application with a PD of 0.25 or above is high risk.

**5.5** The Credit Risk Committee reviews the approval and rejection cut-offs once a year and sets them using the cost of a false approval against the cost of a false rejection.

## 6. Decision Outcomes

**6.1** The system recommends approval only when every eligibility, credit history and affordability rule is met, no manual review trigger applies, and the PD is below the approval cut-off.

**6.2** The system recommends rejection when any rule marked "not eligible" applies, or when the PD is at or above the rejection cut-off.

**6.3** The system recommends manual review in every other case, including any case where a manual review trigger applies.

**6.4** A policy rule marked "not eligible" takes priority over a low PD.

**6.5** A high PD takes priority over an applicant who meets every policy rule.

**6.6** A loan officer must confirm every rejection before it is communicated to the applicant.

## 7. Exceptions and Overrides

**7.1** A loan officer may approve or reject a case sent for manual review, and must record the reason in writing.

**7.2** Overriding a "not eligible" rule requires written approval from a Senior Credit Manager.

**7.3** No override is allowed for a credit score below 600 or a DTI above 0.50.

**7.4** Every override is logged, and the Credit Risk Committee reviews all overrides each quarter.

## 8. Fair Lending

**8.1** The bank does not use gender, religion, caste, marital status or any other protected attribute in the risk model or in any decision rule.

**8.2** The applicant's city or city tier must not be given as a reason for rejection.

**8.3** The bank checks approval rates by gender, age band and city tier every quarter.

**8.4** If the approval rate of any group is below 80 percent of the highest group's rate, the Credit Risk Committee must review the model and the policy.

**8.5** Every rejection must be accompanied by the specific policy reasons, stated in plain language.

**8.6** An explanation must never mention a protected attribute as a factor in the decision.

## 9. Human Review Triggers

**9.1** A loan officer must review every case in which the system recommends rejection.

**9.2** A loan officer must review every case in which the system recommends manual review.

**9.3** A loan officer must review every case with missing or inconsistent data.

**9.4** A loan officer must review every case that no clause in this policy clearly covers.

## 10. Cases Not Covered by This Policy

**10.1** If no clause in this policy applies to the applicant's situation, the explanation must say that the situation is not covered by the policy.

**10.2** In such a case the system must not guess a rule or borrow a rule from another bank, and must route the case to a loan officer.

**10.3** An explanation may cite only clauses that appear in this policy.

## 11. Customer Communication

**11.1** Every decision message must be written in plain language, without technical jargon.

**11.2** A decision message must cite the policy clauses that led to the outcome.

**11.3** A rejected applicant may ask for a review of the decision within 30 days.

**11.4** A rejected applicant may reapply after 90 days.

**11.5** The bank uses applicant data only to assess the loan application and does not share it outside the bank except as required by law.

## 12. Definitions

**12.1** Probability of default (PD) means the model's estimate that the applicant will fail to repay the loan.

**12.2** Manual review means a decision made by a loan officer after reading the application, the model score and the explanation.

**12.3** Delinquency means a missed or late repayment recorded in the applicant's credit history.
